The site read

Four things I'd fix first.

Looked at lulubugsgarden.com from an outside perspective. Four things that would move the needle before anything on the plan below ever ships.

01

The Terms, Privacy, and Cookies links in your footer all 404.

Every page footer shows "Terms and Conditions | Privacy Policy | Cookies Policy" as clickable text, but the links point at your Contact page and the direct URLs (/privacy-policy, /terms-and-conditions, /cookie-policy) return "page not found." That's a real problem: you're running Meta ads that capture PII (name, email) with no privacy policy live. If Meta's compliance team reviews the account, that's grounds to pause your ad account. And it puts you offside on California's CCPA and CPRA. This is the finding that most needs same-day action.

02

The signup forms capture emails but don't send anything back.

"Subscribe Now!" on the homepage, "Join the Club!" on the character page, "Join Now!" on the Join page, and the "bug-mail" contact form all collect names and emails. None of them are wired to Klaviyo, Mailchimp, or any real email tool. Wix's default form just emails the submissions to whoever set it up, which means every parent you sign up drops into someone's inbox and gets nothing back. The site literally promises "Subscribe now to get special bug-mail and get access to weekly new episodes." A promise every signup breaks by silence.

03

Every Meta ad dollar is running blind.

Your homepage doesn't have a Meta Pixel installed. That means the six Meta ads currently running have no way to know which clicks converted, no way to build a lookalike audience from your best viewers, no way to retarget the parents who came to the site but didn't sign up. You're spending on ads with none of the data those ads should be generating. Free to fix. Costs nothing to install. Unlocks every future ad decision.

04

Every social share and every ad preview renders text-only.

None of your pages have an og:image, og:description, or Twitter Card image set. That means every time someone shares a Lulubug's Garden link on iMessage, WhatsApp, LinkedIn, or in a Meta ad, the preview is just the URL and a plain title. No character photo, no color, nothing that would make a parent click. For a brand with strong physical character design, this is a huge missed impression on every share.

And two things I tested myself.

I signed up for bug-mail. I checked my inbox. Nothing came.

Subscribed to the "Join the Garden" form on the homepage. Waited. Signed up again on the Join Now page with a different email. Still waited. Zero welcome email, zero character intro, zero "here's your first episode." The site shows a "Thanks, we received your submission" message on screen, but that's the entire experience. On the back end, no ESP (email service provider) is connected. Every parent who's typed their email into a Lulubug's Garden signup form since April 30 is sitting in a Wix database that never speaks to them.

The platform question

You're on the right platform. Just not wired to work yet.

Every finding above is fixable inside your current setup. Nothing here needs a rebuild, a migration, or a code project. Wix is a real choice for a content-first, media-driven brand like Lulubug's Garden, and the design work already in the editor is solid.

Here's the honest read on where Wix ends and where a fix layer begins.

What Wix already does well for you

  • Fast editor your team can use without a developer. Change images, edit copy, add pages in the browser.
  • Hosting, SSL certificate, mobile responsiveness, and security patches all handled automatically. Nothing for your team to manage.
  • Design flexibility good enough for what a media brand needs. Custom fonts, colors, animations, and a real logo showing correctly on mobile.
  • Wix has a built-in form tool, which is why the "Join the Garden" forms exist at all.

What Wix needs bolted on to actually convert

  • A real email platform (Klaviyo) connected to the Wix forms, so every submission triggers automation instead of dropping into a database.
  • Meta Pixel and GA4 installed properly, so every ad dollar generates measurable data instead of running blind.
  • Real legal pages (Terms, Privacy, Cookies) published as their own URLs, not decoy links, so the ad account stays compliant.
  • Open Graph and Twitter Card images added per page, so every share and every ad preview renders visually instead of text-only.
  • Structured data (JSON-LD) added so Google and AI answer engines can actually cite you.

My recommendation: stay on Wix. Two weeks to bolt on everything above. Everything delivered inside the Wix editor and a few pasted code snippets. Your team can maintain every piece of it after handoff.
