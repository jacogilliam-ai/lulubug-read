What I noticed

Four places the plumbing isn't connected yet.

I read lulubugsgarden.com the way a new parent would, then checked what's underneath. Four things worth fixing before anything else.

01

The Terms, Privacy, and Cookies links in your footer lead to error pages.

Every page footer shows "Terms and Conditions | Privacy Policy | Cookies Policy" as clickable text, but the links point at your Contact page, and the direct web addresses show "page not found." Your forms capture personal information (names and emails) today with no privacy policy live, which puts you out of step with California's privacy laws (CCPA and CPRA). And the moment ads run again, Meta expects a working privacy policy; ad accounts get paused over it. Same-day fix, and it's the first thing I'd ship.

02

The signup forms capture emails but nothing goes back to the parent.

Two pages, two labels ("Join the Club!" on Meet Garden Friends, "Join Now!" on the Join page), one native Wix form underneath, and no email tool connected to it. Submissions land in Wix's default pipeline, which notifies whoever set up the site, and the parent hears nothing. Meanwhile the homepage "Subscribe Now!" button looks like an email signup but links to YouTube. Every signup path is either silent or pointed somewhere else.

03

The podcast is invisible on your own website.

Lulubug's Podcast for Parents has eight episodes on its own YouTube channel, with topics parents search for every night. But there's no page for it on lulubugsgarden.com, no mention of it anywhere on the site, no episode email when a new one drops, and no code that lets Google index it as a podcast. A parent watching the show has no path to the podcast, and a parent searching "helping kids process shame" has no path to you.

Week 1

Stop the leaks.

Klaviyo installed and connected to your forms

Klaviyo is the email tool small kids brands grow on. I install it, connect it to the "Join the Club!" and "Join Now!" forms you already built, and authenticate the sending domain so emails come from a Lulubug's Garden address and don't land in spam. Signups start feeding a list you own the moment this ships.

The last time ads ran, they pointed at YouTube, so the spend bought views on a platform you don't own, and nothing on your site could catch the parents who came looking afterward. I install the Meta Pixel on every page with a signup conversion event on every form. The next campaign starts with tracking on from day one: which ads convert, which parents to re-reach, and lookalike audiences (Meta finds new parents who resemble your best subscribers).

Legal pages installed and published

The footer links lead to error pages today, and fixing that protects the Meta ad account and keeps you inside California's privacy rules. I install standard, lawyer-vetted templates (Wix's built-in generator or Termly.io), publish them at the proper web addresses, and wire the footer links so they work. Your attorney reviews everything before it goes fully live. You own the final language.

Home hero contradiction fixed

The homepage says "Coming soon to your screens!" directly above a "Watch now on YouTube!" button, for a show with 91 videos live. I fix the copy to one consistent message and align the button with the truth: episodes are here, watch now.

Orphan product page unpublished

A leftover Wix Store test page is live in your sitemap with an "Add to Cart" button and empty prices. I unpublish it so Google stops seeing a store that isn't real.

Three related setups so search engines can find your new pages and every signup, download, and visit is measurable.
