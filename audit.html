Full audit

What jumped out across every page.

Thirty-three findings across seven categories. Ranked by impact. Re-checked against the live site in August 2026.

Everything below is fixable inside the setup you already have. None of it touches what's working: the show, the pace, the team. This list exists so nothing leaks while that compounds.

01 / Critical

Five fixes a visitor would feel this week.

High visibility, high urgency, all fixable inside the Wix editor.

01

Terms, Privacy, and Cookies footer links all lead to error pages.

Every page footer displays "Terms and Conditions | Privacy Policy | Cookies Policy" as clickable text, but the link points to your Contact page, and the direct addresses (/privacy-policy, /terms-and-conditions, /cookie-policy) show "page not found." For a site that collects names and emails and has run Meta ads, that's a real gap: a privacy policy is table stakes for email collection, and Meta expects advertisers to have a working one the moment campaigns resume. Same-day fix.

02

Orphan product page live at /product-page/printable-packet-episode-1.

The page loads normally, is included in the Wix product sitemap, has empty price fields, and shows a working "Add to Cart" button. Left over from the Wix Stores app installation. The site has no store, but Google can see one. Same-day fix (unpublish).

03

Home hero contradicts itself.

"Coming soon to your screens!" sits directly above "Watch now on YouTube!" for a show with 95 videos live. It confuses every parent who lands there. Same-day copy fix.

04

Brigham Taylor filmography is mangled.

Film titles render as bare ALL-CAPS strings with orphan connecting words ("with director Jon Favreau,", "franchise,"). TOKYO COWBOY and WHISKEY THROTTLE (his recent independent films) are unlabeled and mixed in with Disney tentpoles. His name is one of the site's strongest legitimacy signals, and this presentation sells him short. One-day copy rewrite.

05

Dead Wix apps installed with zero surface.

Wix Members Area, Wix Events and Tickets, Wix Stores, and ShoutOut Legacy are all enabled on the site with no user-facing surface. ShoutOut is Wix's deprecated email tool. If the Join form is silently wired to it, every subscriber vanishes into a legacy database Wix no longer supports. One-day cleanup.

02 / Missing infrastructure

Nothing captures. Nothing measures.

Five items. Every signup, and every future ad dollar, runs blind until these ship.

01

No Meta Pixel base tag anywhere in the HTML.

When your Meta ads were live (the Ad Library showed six running at one point), no Pixel was installed, so none of that traffic was tracked: no conversion data, no lookalike audiences, no list of parents to re-reach. Free to fix, and worth installing now so the next campaign starts with tracking on from day one.

02

Google Tag Manager container loaded, but no verifiable Google Analytics (GA4) events.

Tag container GTM-P2BL8D44 is loaded on every page, but I can't confirm what's inside the container from outside the site. Without confirmed Google Analytics events, there's no way to see how many people land on the site, how many sign up, or where they leave. Half-day fix to verify the setup and add missing events.

03

No connected email service provider.

No Klaviyo, Mailchimp, ConvertKit, Beehiiv, or Wix Ascend markers detected anywhere in the site's code. Every signup submission lands in the default Wix form pipeline, which notifies whoever set up the site while the parent hears nothing. Explains exactly what I saw when I tested the signup myself: two submissions, zero emails back. Two to three days to wire a real email tool and draft the welcome automation.

04

No TikTok pixel or YouTube subscribe button.

Lulubug's Garden already posts on TikTok, which means a TikTok Pixel would let you eventually run TikTok ads with conversion tracking already in place. A YouTube subscribe button on your site would let a parent subscribe to the channel without leaving your page. Both optional now, both easy to add once the email tool and Meta Pixel are in.

05

JSON-LD structured data is one line.

Sitewide schema is a bare WebSite entry. Missing: Organization for Lulubug Enterprises LLC, Person entries for Jil Hunter and Brigham Taylor and Dr. Karen Manotas, VideoObject per episode, and a PodcastSeries entry for Lulubug's Podcast for Parents. Without this, Google and AI answer engines have no anchor for "who is Lulubug" questions.

03 / Content gaps

Pages that should exist but don't.

Six items. Every one is a missed SEO landing and a missed conversion opportunity.

01

No per-character detail pages.

Lulubug, Curby, Sterling, Willow, and Daisy each get a homepage card but no dedicated URL. That's five missed SEO landing pages for character-name searches ("who is Lulubug," "Curby caterpillar"), and five missed pages where a parent could dig deeper before signing up.

02

The podcast lives on its own YouTube channel and is invisible on lulubugsgarden.com.

Lulubug's Podcast for Parents has 8 episodes (16 to 38 minutes) on topics parents actually search: "why your child seems ungrateful," "helping kids process shame," "why small disappointments build stronger kids," plus a Kaycee Rasmussen interview. But the podcast channel has 20 subscribers while the main show channel has 1,380. Zero cross-promotion between the two. Zero mentions of the podcast anywhere on lulubugsgarden.com (checked home, videos, and about pages), and no podcast code for Google. The show is even distributed right, hosted on a real podcast platform and live on Apple Podcasts and the other apps, and the website doesn't link to any of it. The biggest single content opportunity sitting untouched on the site.

03

No proper videos hub.

The address /videos is actually the character intro page, not a videos gallery. The nav labels it "Meet Garden Friends" while the web address says /videos. Visitors and Google both get confused.

04

No press or media page.

Parade and Yahoo Entertainment covered the launch, but a visitor to lulubugsgarden.com would never know. Brigham Taylor's Disney background and Dr. Karen Manotas's University of Utah credentials are the two strongest press hooks Lulubug has, and right now they're buried on the About page. A proper Press page (the coverage so far, photos, quotes, high-res logos, contact for interviews) turns two headlines into a compounding story.

05

No blog or parent resources.

No content ranks for "help kids name emotions," "social emotional learning ages 3 to 7," or any of the hundreds of parenting-education queries Lulubug is uniquely positioned to answer. Ten well-written parent-education posts would build a compounding SEO moat over six to twelve months.

06

No FAQ, no For Educators, no newsletter archive.

Standard trust pages for a kids-education brand are all missing. A For Educators page especially would open the door to school and preschool licensing conversations, which is exactly where kids brands at this stage grow next.

04 / Metadata and SEO

The layer only Google sees.

Five items. Invisible to visitors, decisive for Google and shared-link previews.

01

Every page missing meta description, og:description, og:image, twitter:description, twitter:image.

When anyone shares a Lulubug's Garden link on iMessage, WhatsApp, Slack, LinkedIn, or Instagram Stories, the preview renders text-only. No image. No description. Just the address. For a brand with this much character design, every text-only preview is a missed impression.

02

twitter:card meta tag makes a promise the page doesn't keep.

Twitter Cards are the visual previews that show up when someone shares a Lulubug's Garden link on Twitter or X. Your site has twitter:card="summary_large_image" set, which tells X to expect a large image preview, but no image is actually provided (twitter:image is missing). Result: broken preview on X. Kids brands don't live on X, but educators and journalists sharing about the show will hit this. Five-minute cleanup: either add the image or remove the tag entirely.

03

Page titles are formulaic and keyword-free.

Every page title reads as "[Page] | Lulubug". Missing keywords parents actually search: "kids emotional learning show," "social emotional cartoon," "kids mental health tools." Google-friendly titles need to describe what the page is, not just name it.

04

Sitemap advertises empty store-products and store-categories entries.

The Wix-generated sitemap includes store-products-sitemap.xml and store-categories-sitemap.xml, both empty. Google reads this as "store with no products," a weak signal for a brand not trying to sell anything yet. Cleanup: disable the Wix Stores app entirely.

05

Favicon is default Wix pfavico.ico.

Every browser tab, every bookmark, every search result loses the brand mark. Fifteen minutes to build a proper favicon set (16, 32, 180, 192, 512) using the Lulubug logo and upload to Wix.

05 / Signup labels and misdirected buttons

Signup flow is scattered. One button points the wrong way.

Five items. Different labels for the same action across pages, plus one button that points somewhere else entirely.

01

Homepage "Subscribe Now!" button actually links to YouTube.

In the "Join the Garden!" section on the homepage, the "Subscribe Now!" button looks visually like an email newsletter signup, but the href points to your YouTube channel. Any parent clicking that button expecting to enter their email gets pulled off your site to YouTube instead. Same misdirection on the hero "Watch Now!" button, which at least makes sense in context, but the near-footer "Subscribe Now!" is confusing because the section around it talks about updates and news. Fix: either change the button to actually open the signup form, or change the button label to "Watch on YouTube" so the CTA matches the destination.

02

"Join the Club!" (Meet Garden Friends page).

The character page calls the signup "Join the Club!" and pitches it as access to Lulubug's Feelings Flower community. Different label from every other signup entry point, framed differently, but underneath it's the same Wix form.

03

"Join Now!" (Join Now page CTA button).

The dedicated Join page calls the same signup "Join Now!" as the CTA text on the submit button. Third label for the same action.

04

"Subscribe for updates and to join the adventure!" (Join page body copy).

Right above the same signup form on the Join page, the body copy adds a fourth framing. Same form, fourth version of what it's for.

05

"bug-mail" (brand term + Contact form).

The brand's own name for the newsletter is "bug-mail" (the best one). But the Contact form is ALSO called "bug-mail," which confuses parents about whether they're subscribing or asking a question. Pick one label ("bug-mail" for the newsletter, "Contact Us" for the form), unify sitewide.

06 / Content quality

Little fixes that read as care.

Four items. Details that pile up.

01

Typography inconsistent across pages.

The About page uses double-hyphens (--) as em-dash substitutes. The Videos page uses proper em dashes. Wix's editor allows both; readers notice both. Standardize on real punctuation.

02

Hidden accessibility text is showing on the page.

"top of page" and "bottom of page" appear as visible page copy on multiple pages. These are Wix's built-in accessibility helpers, meant for screen readers only, and they should never be visible. Wix Editor setting fix.

03

Image alts are default camera filenames.

Homepage image alts include DSC_4864.jpg, Dianas_visuals_4988-Edit.jpg, Screenshot 2026-03-16 at 3.55.54 PM.png, and FM00074431_edited_edited.jpg. Zero SEO signal. Zero accessibility value. Google Image Search cannot rank any of them properly.

04

Hero video file has a double extension.

The homepage hero video file is named Lulubug Theme Song. Compressedmp4.mp4. Cosmetic, but reads as a rushed export uploaded without cleanup.

07 / Small but real

Three quick ones to close it out.

Small on their own, cumulative in effect.

01

Wix placeholder text left in the page code.

The page code contains <meta http-equiv="etag" content="bug"/>, a piece of Wix placeholder text that was never cleaned up. Cosmetic, but every crawler that inspects the page sees it.

02

No custom 404 page.

Hitting any dead link shows the default Wix "page not found" screen. A custom version with a Lulubug character, a "Bug got lost" message, and links back to key pages is a 30-minute fix that saves every misclicked visitor.

03

The logo's image description shows code instead of an apostrophe.

The site logo's image description reads Lulubug&#x27;s-Garden-logo, with code where the apostrophe should be. Screen readers read that code out loud to visually impaired visitors. Wix Editor fix.
