Full audit

What jumped out across every page.

Thirty-five findings across seven categories. Ranked by launch-timed impact. Snapshot in time as of the audit date.

01 / Critical

Things shipped visibly broken.

Six items. High visibility, high urgency, all fixable inside the Wix editor.

01

Terms, Privacy, and Cookies footer links all 404.

Every page footer displays "Terms and Conditions | Privacy Policy | Cookies Policy" as clickable text, but the href points to /contact. Direct URLs (/privacy-policy, /terms-and-conditions, /cookie-policy) return HTTP 404. For a site running Meta ads and capturing PII (email, first name), this is a Meta ad policy risk plus CCPA and CPRA compliance exposure. Same-day fix.

02

Orphan product page live at /product-page/printable-packet-episode-1.

Returns HTTP 200, is included in the Wix product sitemap, has empty Regular Price and Sale Price fields, and shows a working "Add to Cart" button. Left over from Wix Stores app installation. The site claims no store exists, but Google can see one. Same-day fix (unpublish).

03

Home hero contradicts itself.

"Coming soon to your screens!" sits directly above "Watch now on YouTube!" for a show with live episodes. Undermines the launch narrative and confuses every parent landing there from a Meta ad. Same-day copy fix.

04

About-page bios truncated mid-sentence.

Jil's bio starts at "she realized that..." with the opening missing. Dr. Karen Manotas's bio cuts at "Department of Pediat..." Sterling's character bio (on the Videos page) cuts at "Sterling's steady". Wix text-box overflow bug. Same-day fix by resizing containers.

05

Brigham Taylor filmography is mangled.

Film titles render as bare ALL-CAPS strings with orphan connecting words ("with director Jon Favreau,", "franchise,"). TOKYO COWBOY and WHISKEY THROTTLE (his recent independent films) are unlabeled and mixed in with Disney tentpoles. His name is the site's strongest legitimacy signal, and this section reads amateur. One-day copy rewrite.

06

Dead Wix apps installed with zero surface.

Wix Members Area, Wix Events and Tickets, Wix Stores, and ShoutOut Legacy are all enabled on the site with no user-facing surface. ShoutOut is Wix's deprecated email tool. If the Join form is silently wired to it, every subscriber vanishes into a legacy database Wix no longer supports. One-day cleanup.

02 / Missing infrastructure

Nothing captures. Nothing measures.

Five items. Every ad dollar and every signup runs blind until these ship.

01

No Meta Pixel base tag anywhere in the HTML.

Running six live Meta ads with no Pixel installed means no conversion tracking, no lookalike audiences, no ability to retarget parents who visited the site but didn't sign up. Every ad decision from here on is guesswork. Free to fix. Costs nothing to install.

02

Google Tag Manager container loaded, but no verifiable GA4 events.

GTM container GTM-P2BL8D44 is loaded on every page, but the container's contents aren't verifiable server-side. Without confirmed GA4 events, there's no way to see how many people are landing on the site, how many are signing up, or where they're bouncing. Half-day fix to verify the GA4 setup and add missing events.

03

No connected email service provider.

No Klaviyo, Mailchimp, ConvertKit, Beehiiv, or Wix Ascend markers detected anywhere in the site's code. Every "Subscribe Now" and "Join the Club" submission is landing in the default Wix Form pipeline, which delivers email notifications to whoever set up the site. Explains the "signup doesn't fire" report perfectly. Two to three days to wire an ESP and draft the welcome automation.

04

No TikTok pixel or YouTube subscribe SDK.

Lulubug's Garden already posts on TikTok, which means a TikTok Pixel would let you eventually run TikTok Ads with conversion tracking already in place. A YouTube subscribe button SDK on your site would let a parent subscribe to the channel without leaving your page. Both optional now, both easy to add once the ESP and Meta Pixel are in.

05

JSON-LD structured data is one line.

Sitewide schema is a bare WebSite entry. Missing: Organization for Lulubug Enterprises LLC, Person entries for Jil Hunter and Brigham Taylor and Dr. Karen Manotas, VideoObject per episode, and a PodcastSeries if the parent-facing segment is ever spun off. Without this, Google and AI answer engines have no anchor for "who is Lulubug" queries.

03 / Content gaps

Pages that should exist but don't.

Six items. Every one is a missed SEO landing and a missed conversion opportunity.

01

No per-character detail pages.

Lulubug, Curby, Sterling, Willow, and Daisy each get a homepage card but no dedicated URL. That's five missed SEO landing pages for character-name searches ("who is Lulubug," "Curby caterpillar"), and five missed pages where a parent could dig deeper before signing up.

02

No podcast or parent-segment page.

The parent-facing segment at the end of each YouTube episode has no dedicated landing page. That's a missed opportunity to package Dr. Karen Manotas's clinical guidance as a separately-discoverable content strand.

03

No proper videos hub.

The URL /videos is actually the character intro page, not a videos gallery. The nav labels it "Meet Garden Friends" while the URL slug is /videos. Users and Google both get confused.

04

No press or media page.

Brigham Taylor's Disney background and Dr. Karen Manotas's University of Utah credentials are the two strongest press hooks Lulubug has. Right now they're buried on the About page. A proper Press page (photos, quotes, high-res logos, contact for interviews) unlocks earned media coverage.

05

No blog or parent resources.

No content ranks for "help kids name emotions," "social emotional learning ages 3 to 7," or any of the hundreds of parenting-education queries Lulubug is uniquely positioned to answer. Ten well-written parent-education posts would build a compounding SEO moat over six to twelve months.

06

No FAQ, no For Educators, no newsletter archive.

Standard trust pages for a kids-education brand are all missing. A For Educators page especially would open the door to school and preschool licensing conversations, which is exactly where kids brands at this stage grow next.

04 / Metadata and SEO

The technical foundation isn't set up.

Five items. Invisible to visitors, decisive for Google and shared-link previews.

01

Every page missing meta description, og:description, og:image, twitter:description, twitter:image.

When anyone shares a Lulubug's Garden URL on iMessage, WhatsApp, Slack, LinkedIn, Instagram Stories, or in a Meta ad, the link preview renders text-only. No image. No description. Just the URL. For a brand with strong physical character design, this is a huge missed impression on every single share.

02

twitter:card meta tag makes a promise the page doesn't keep.

Twitter Cards are the visual previews that show up when someone shares a Lulubug's Garden link on Twitter or X. Your site has twitter:card="summary_large_image" set, which tells X to expect a large image preview, but no image is actually provided (twitter:image is missing). Result: broken preview on X. Kids brands don't live on X, but educators and journalists sharing about the show will hit this. Five-minute cleanup: either add the image or remove the tag entirely.

03

Page titles are formulaic and keyword-free.

Every page title reads as "[Page] | Lulubug". Missing keywords parents actually search: "kids emotional learning show," "social emotional cartoon," "kids mental health tools." Google-friendly titles need to describe what the page is, not just name it.

04

Sitemap advertises empty store-products and store-categories entries.

The Wix-generated sitemap includes store-products-sitemap.xml and store-categories-sitemap.xml, both empty. Google reads this as "store with no products," a weak signal for a brand not trying to sell anything yet. Cleanup: disable the Wix Stores app entirely.

05

Favicon is default Wix pfavico.ico.

Every browser tab, every bookmark, every search result loses the brand mark. Fifteen minutes to build a proper favicon set (16, 32, 180, 192, 512) using the Lulubug logo and upload to Wix.

05 / Signup labels and misdirected buttons

Signup flow is scattered. One button lies.

Five items. Different labels for the same action across pages, plus one misleading button.

01

Homepage "Subscribe Now!" button actually links to YouTube.

In the "Join the Garden!" section on the homepage, the "Subscribe Now!" button looks visually like an email newsletter signup, but the href points to your YouTube channel. Any parent clicking that button expecting to enter their email gets pulled off your site to YouTube instead. Same misdirection on the hero "Watch Now!" button, which at least makes sense in context, but the near-footer "Subscribe Now!" is confusing because the section around it talks about updates and news. Fix: either change the button to actually open the signup form, or change the button label to "Watch on YouTube" so the CTA matches the destination.

02

"Join the Club!" (Meet Garden Friends page).

The character page calls the signup "Join the Club!" and pitches it as access to Lulubug's Feelings Flower community. Different label from every other signup entry point, framed differently, but underneath it's the same Wix form.

03

"Join Now!" (Join Now page CTA button).

The dedicated Join page calls the same signup "Join Now!" as the CTA text on the submit button. Third label for the same action.

04

"Subscribe for updates and to join the adventure!" (Join page body copy).

Right above the same signup form on the Join page, the body copy adds a fourth framing. Same form, fourth version of what it's for.

05

"bug-mail" (brand term + Contact form).

The brand's own name for the newsletter is "bug-mail" (the best one). But the Contact form is ALSO called "bug-mail," which confuses parents about whether they're subscribing or asking a question. Pick one label ("bug-mail" for the newsletter, "Contact Us" for the form), unify sitewide.

06 / Content quality

Small copy and typography issues.

Four items. Details that pile up.

01

Typography inconsistent across pages.

The About page uses double-hyphens (--) as em-dash substitutes. The Videos page uses proper em dashes. Wix's editor allows both; readers notice both. Standardize on real punctuation.

02

Wix accessibility skip-nav strings render as visible text.

"top of page" and "bottom of page" appear as visible page copy on multiple pages. These are Wix's built-in skip-nav a11y helpers, and they should never be visible. Wix Editor setting fix.

03

Image alts are default camera filenames.

Homepage image alts include DSC_4864.jpg, Dianas_visuals_4988-Edit.jpg, Screenshot 2026-03-16 at 3.55.54 PM.png, and FM00074431_edited_edited.jpg. Zero SEO signal. Zero accessibility value. Google Image Search cannot rank any of them properly.

04

Hero video file has a double extension.

The homepage hero video file is named Lulubug Theme Song. Compressedmp4.mp4. Cosmetic, but reads as a rushed export uploaded without cleanup.

07 / Small but real

One more pass.

Four items. Small on their own, cumulative in effect.

01

Wix placeholder ETag tag shipped to production.

The HTML head contains <meta http-equiv="etag" content="bug"/>: Wix placeholder text left in the head. Cosmetic, but every crawler that inspects the page sees it.

02

Only four total publishes.

The X-Wix-Published-Version header confirms the site has only been published four times ever. Not a defect on its own, but it means very little iteration has happened. The team is early. Small edits ship carefully; big changes probably feel scary.

03

No custom 404 page.

Hitting any dead URL shows the default Wix "page not found" screen. Custom 404 with a Lulubug character, a "Bug got lost" message, and links back to key pages would be a 30-minute fix that saves every misclicked visitor.

04

Escaped-apostrophe alt text on the logo.

The site logo's image alt renders as Lulubug&#x27;s-Garden-logo (HTML-entity-encoded apostrophe). Screen readers announce the entity code, not the apostrophe. Wix Editor fix.
